Agents in Production: Evals, Harnesses, and the Reinvention of Recommendation

Agents are live. Most fail quietly. How do we make them reliable, measurable, and efficient at scale?

Sun, Dec 6, 2026 6:00 – 7:00 PM Parkside 2 · ICC Sydney
Why now

Three problems, one proving ground

Agents fail quietly

Errors compound across steps, cost grows per tool call, and offline benchmarks rarely predict production.

Evaluate the trajectory

Not single outputs. Harnesses and simulators that reflect production; SFT and RL on traces; cost-aware routing.

RecSys as the test case

When systems understand goals and take actions, recommendation shifts from predicting clicks to achieving objectives.

?

Will agents replace the retrieve → rank → recommend stack, or rebuild it?
